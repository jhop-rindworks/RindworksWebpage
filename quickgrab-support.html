QuickGrab Support

QuickGrab (on the App Store as QuickGrab: Screen Capture) is a screenshot, screen recording and annotation app for Mac, made by RindWorks.

Contact us

Email hello@rindworks.com with your question. If something isn't working, tell us your macOS version and the steps that led to the problem. You can also attach the app's error log: open Settings ▸ Storage ▸ Diagnostic log. It lists the errors and the names of the files involved, never your captures.

Common questions

Captures show only my desktop background

macOS hasn't allowed QuickGrab to record the screen yet. Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on QuickGrab, then quit and reopen the app.

Where are my captures?

In Pictures ▸ QuickGrab, unless you chose another folder in Settings ▸ Storage. Your annotations are kept in a hidden .annotations folder there, so markups stay editable when you reopen a capture.

A hotkey doesn't work

The defaults are:

  • ⌘⇧2 Region
  • ⌘⇧1 Full screen
  • ⌘⌥⇧W Window
  • ⌘⌥⇧2 Repeat last region
  • ⌘⌥⇧T Copy text from the screen

Another app may already use the same keys. Pick different ones in Settings ▸ Capture ▸ Edit Hotkeys…. To use ⌘⇧3, ⌘⇧4 or ⌘⇧5, first turn off the Mac's own screenshot shortcuts in System Settings ▸ Keyboard ▸ Keyboard Shortcuts ▸ Screenshots.

The microphone or camera doesn't turn on in a recording

Allow QuickGrab in System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Microphone or Camera. Recording your Mac's own sound (🔊) needs macOS 13 or later.

Send to… says my credentials can't be read

Edit the destination in Settings ▸ Sharing and enter the key or token again. This happens when the Mac's Keychain entry was reset, or the settings came from another Mac.

My company manages QuickGrab

IT can set and lock some settings with a configuration profile for the preference domain com.rindworks.quickgrab. Locked settings say "Set by your organization". Email us for the list of settings and an example profile.
